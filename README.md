‎# 🍪 Cookies & Web Security Awareness

## 📌 What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser that contain data such as session identifiers. They are used by websites to recognize users and improve browsing experience.

Cookies are created by the server and stored locally in your browser.

---

## 🌐 How Cookies Work

- You visit a website
- The server sends a cookie to your browser
- Your browser stores it locally
- The cookie is sent back with each request
- The server uses it to identify your session

---

## ⚙️ Why Cookies Are Important

Cookies are commonly used for:
- Keeping users logged in
- Saving preferences
- Improving user experience
- Maintaining sessions (e.g. shopping carts)

---

## ⚠️ Security Considerations

Although cookies themselves are not harmful, they can become a security risk if exposed.

If session cookies are stolen, attackers may attempt to impersonate a user session without needing a password.

---

## 🛡️ How to Stay Safe

- Never share your session cookies or browser data
- Avoid running unknown scripts or tools
- Use updated and secure browsers
- Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
- Regularly clear cookies for sensitive accounts
